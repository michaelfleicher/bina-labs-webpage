## Agentic Commerce (2026)

Relevant for e-commerce operators.
- **OpenAI ACP** (Sept 2025, Stripe payment rails) - Etsy, Shopify, Walmart integrated
- **Shopify + Google UCP** - REST/MCP/AP2/A2A
- **Nuance**: OpenAI scaled back native Instant Checkout early 2026 (~30 merchants); pivoted to in-ChatGPT retailer apps
- **Implication**: Structured Product + Offer schema with price/availability/GTIN now matters MORE than native checkout integration. Make products machine-readable first; native commerce integrations second.
